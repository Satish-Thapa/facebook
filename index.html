Clipper Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Clipper is a tool that makes short vertical videos and news or fact posts, and publishes them to social media accounts that belong to the person who runs it. This policy explains what data Clipper uses, why, where it is kept and how to delete it.

In short: Clipper only acts on accounts its owner connects. It runs on the owner's own computer, keeps data there, does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

1. Who we are

Clipper is operated by its owner (the "operator"). Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

2. Data we use from Facebook and Instagram

When the operator connects a Facebook Page and its linked Instagram professional account, Clipper receives and uses:

  • Access tokens that Facebook issues for the connected Page and Instagram account, so Clipper can post on their behalf.
  • Page and account identifiers (Page ID, Page name, Instagram account ID and username), to know where to post.
  • Basic results of our own posts (post IDs, links, view and play counts), to show the operator how the posts are doing.

Clipper uses these only to:

  • publish videos, photos and captions that the operator created or approved to the connected Page and Instagram account;
  • show the operator links to those posts and how many views they got.

Clipper does not read private messages, friend lists, other people's profiles or posts, and does not collect data about people who view or interact with the posts.

3. Other platforms

In the same way, Clipper can connect to the operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts to upload videos and read view counts of those uploads. It uses the data those platforms return only for that purpose. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Where data is stored

  • Everything is stored locally on the operator's computer (a local database and files). Clipper has no central server that collects this data.
  • Access tokens are kept on that computer only and are hidden from logs.
  • To write captions and pick moments, Clipper may send video titles, transcripts or news text (not access tokens or personal data of other people) to an AI service the operator chooses. This can be a model on the operator's own computer or network, or a provider such as DeepSeek.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. Data is only sent to the platform it belongs to (for example, a video is sent to Facebook to be posted on the connected Page).

6. How long we keep data

Access tokens are kept until the account is disconnected or the token expires. Post links and view counts are kept until the operator deletes them or resets the app. Source videos are deleted after the clips are made.

7. How to delete your data

You can remove Clipper's access and delete its data at any time:

  • Remove access on Facebook: go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find Clipper and click Remove. Its tokens stop working straight away.
  • Remove access on Instagram: in Instagram go to Settings → Apps and websites and remove Clipper.
  • Delete the stored data: in Clipper, disconnect the account on the Socials page, or use Settings → Reset everything to delete all stored tokens, post records, view counts and logs.
  • Ask us: email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] with the subject "Delete my data" and we will delete everything related to your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Posts already published on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or TikTok are not deleted by Clipper; you can delete them on the platform itself.

8. Children

Clipper is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
